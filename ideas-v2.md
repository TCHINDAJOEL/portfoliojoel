# Portfolio Design - Version 2: Modern Dark Elegance

## Design Philosophy: Modern Dark Elegance with Circular Focus

### Core Characteristics:
1. **Dark Mode Foundation** - Deep charcoal/black background (#1a1a1a or #0f0f0f)
2. **Circular Photo** - Professional headshot in large circular frame with subtle glow
3. **Asymmetric Layout** - Photo on right, content on left with dynamic spacing
4. **Purple/Violet Accents** - Modern accent color for highlights and CTAs
5. **Geometric Elements** - Decorative circles and lines for visual interest

### Color Palette:
- **Background**: Deep charcoal (#1a1a1a) or pure black (#0f0f0f)
- **Text Primary**: White (#ffffff)
- **Text Secondary**: Light gray (#b0b0b0)
- **Accent**: Purple/Violet (#a78bfa or #c084fc)
- **Highlights**: Soft purple glow effects

### Layout Structure:
- **Navigation**: Top bar with logo, menu items, search, login
- **Hero Section**: 
  - Left: Title, subtitle, description, CTA button
  - Right: Large circular photo with decorative elements
  - Decorative circles in corners
- **Sections Below**: About, Services/Skills, Contact

### Typography:
- **Headings**: Bold, modern sans-serif (Poppins or Montserrat)
- **Body**: Clean sans-serif (Inter or Lato)
- **Accent Text**: Purple highlights on key words

### Visual Elements:
- Circular photo frame with subtle shadow/glow
- Decorative geometric circles (top-left, bottom-right)
- Diagonal lines or curves for visual flow
- Smooth transitions and hover effects
- Icons for skills/services

### Interaction:
- Smooth hover effects on buttons
- Subtle animations on scroll
- Interactive skill badges
- Smooth navigation with anchor links
